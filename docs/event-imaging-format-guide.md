# Choose the Right Event Imaging Format

ChackTok supports multiple event imaging workflows. Choose the format by the deliverable, venue, guest flow, staffing, and safety requirements rather than by equipment novelty alone.

## Format Comparison

| Format | Best suited to | Typical deliverable | Operating considerations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 360 Photo Booth | High-energy group experiences and social content | Orbiting video and slow-motion edits | Platform space, queue control, supervision |
| iPad Photo Booth | Portable, high-turnover, or self-service events | Photo, GIF, short video, optional print | Mount, lighting, power, network |
| Magic Mirror Photo Booth | Interactive full-length capture | Photo, GIF, branded guest media | Screen placement, guest instructions, lighting |
| Robotic camera arm | Premium activations where capture is part of the show | Motion-synchronized cinematic clip | Trained operator, safety boundary, large footprint |
| Full-body 3D scanner | Personalized digital or physical products | Full-body digital model or printed figure | Controlled capture area, processing expectations, fulfillment |

## Shared Workflow Layer

ChackTok provides a common event imaging system across supported formats. Depending on the selected hardware, format, and app version, the available workflow can include:

- Event templates and client branding.
- Supported hardware interaction and capture.
- Media processing and visual effects.
- QR code, email, SMS, gallery, social, or print delivery where available.
- Offline task queuing for eligible uploads.
- Basic guest data and engagement reporting where enabled.

Do not assume that every capability applies to every format. Confirm the exact control, processing, delivery, and reporting functions for the selected configuration. A common system can reduce retraining, but it does not remove format-specific setup, safety, or production requirements.

## Selection Questions

Before choosing a format, confirm:

1. What must the guest receive: a photo, video, cinematic clip, digital model, or physical product?
2. How much floor space and setup time does the venue allow?
3. Will the experience be self-service or attended?
4. What guest volume must the workflow handle?
5. Is live digital delivery required?
6. Are printing, post-processing, or later fulfillment included?
7. What safety boundary and operator training are required?
8. What result will be reported to the client?

## Start Small

Test one complete booking workflow before expanding a fleet:

- Use the actual capture hardware and event template.
- Measure setup time and guest throughput.
- Test the delivery path under weak network conditions.
- Confirm operator recovery steps.
- Record client approval, consent, and reporting requirements.
