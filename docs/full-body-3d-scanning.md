# Full-Body 3D Scanning Workflow

This guide explains how to evaluate and prepare a ChackTok full-body 3D scanning workflow for commercial events and personalized imaging services.

Viewing locations, local installations, and delivery schedules can change. Confirm current availability directly with ChackTok before promising an on-site demonstration or regional delivery.

## What the Workflow Produces

A full-body 3D scanning workflow is designed to capture a subject through a controlled 360-degree process and generate a full-body digital model.

Depending on the selected production service, the model can support:

- Personalized digital keepsakes.
- Customized 3D-printed figures.
- Animation or visualization assets.
- Other applications that require a full-body digital model.

Confirm the promised file, preview, revision, and physical-product deliverables before accepting a booking.

## Workflow Stages

1. Define the final digital or physical deliverable.
2. Prepare the scanner, capture area, lighting, power, and network.
3. Explain subject positioning and movement requirements.
4. Run the supported 360-degree capture workflow.
5. Check the captured image set before releasing the subject.
6. Complete the configured model-processing workflow.
7. Review the returned model.
8. Deliver the digital asset or continue to approved 3D-print fulfillment.

Processing and fulfillment can continue after the live event. Set clear expectations for preview timing and final delivery.

## Site and Guest Planning

Before a commercial booking:

- Reserve a controlled capture area around the scanner.
- Keep the platform and subject area clear.
- Confirm subject height, pose, clothing, and accessibility considerations.
- Test lighting, power, connectivity, and the complete capture sequence.
- Define who checks capture quality and who approves the model.
- Prepare a recapture process.
- Explain data handling, retention, and delivery to the client and subject.

## Proof Before Sale

Operators evaluating the workflow should ask to see:

- A complete capture session rather than only a product rendering.
- Example source captures and finished models.
- Expected processing and fulfillment times.
- The recapture and quality-review process.
- Current system configuration and regional availability.
- Support responsibilities for the scanner, software workflow, and output.
