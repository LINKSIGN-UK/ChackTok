# ChackTok Event Workflow Planning

ChackTok brings event setup, templates, effects, content creation, and guest sharing into one software workflow. Use the planning guides below to prepare the content, equipment, operator handoff, and delivery steps for your event.

## Weddings and Private Events

### Define the Guest Experience

Agree on the type of photo or video the guest will create, the event branding, and the way guests will receive their content. Include the venue layout, lighting, available power, and network access in the event brief.

### Prepare the Event

1. Gather the client's approved artwork and content requirements.
2. Prepare the event settings, template, and effects.
3. Record the complete camera, software device, and booth-controller configuration.
4. Create a sample with the selected settings and review it with the client.
5. Rehearse the guest-delivery sequence on the venue network.

### Plan the Operator Handoff

Keep the setup record, approved sample, opening checks, and support contact together. Assign responsibility for welcoming guests, supervising equipment, and helping with delivery.

## Corporate Events and Brand Activations

### Define the Campaign Deliverable

Start with the content the client wants guests to create. Agree on the artwork, output format, delivery path, and any requested reporting fields before selecting the equipment and software configuration.

### Prepare the Content and Delivery

1. Collect approved brand assets and the client's content brief.
2. Prepare the event template and effects.
3. Review a sample through the complete capture-to-delivery path.
4. Confirm the handling requirements for guest information and media.
5. Record the delivery method and the person responsible for the client handoff.

### Measure the Event

Choose the measures that answer the client's question. Record the source, counting method, and collection process for each measure. Keep session counts, delivered items, and guest feedback as separate measures when reviewing results.

## Rental Teams and Multiple Setups

### Keep a Record for Each Complete Setup

Keep one configuration record for each complete capture-to-delivery setup, identifying which device performs capture, booth control, and guest delivery. Use that complete setup record for the configuration review.

### Prepare the Team

1. Assign an operator and equipment list to each booking.
2. Keep the event artwork, selected settings, and approved sample with that booking.
3. Rehearse the workflow on each assigned setup.
4. Check account access and the permissions needed for each operator.
5. Record the client-delivery steps and the person completing them.

### Review the Booking

Retain the configuration, observed results, support issues, and client feedback. Use those records to update the next event's preparation checklist.

## Build Your Event Brief

| Item | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Event | Venue, layout, guest flow, and operator responsibilities |
| Content | Capture format, artwork, template, effects, and approved sample |
| Configuration | Software device, OS, ChackTok version, camera, controller, and connections |
| Delivery | Guest access steps, network, client handoff, and delivery owner |
| Review | Observed results, issue details, and follow-up actions |
