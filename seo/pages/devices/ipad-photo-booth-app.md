# iPad Photo Booth App for Events

ChackTok supports iPad Photo Booth workflows from event setup and capture through branded processing, printing, digital delivery, and basic event reporting where the selected device, camera, printer, and app version are compatible.

## When an iPad Booth Workflow Fits

iPad booth setups are useful for weddings, parties, retail activations, registration-adjacent experiences, and smaller venues where a full 360 platform is not required. Operators should confirm device model, app version, storage, camera permissions, stand stability, network, and charging before the event.

## Capture Options

Operators can use the built-in iPad camera or a compatible external camera workflow. DSLR cameras and mainstream action cameras such as GoPro and DJI can be used where the model, firmware, connection method, and app version are supported.

Choose the simplest capture configuration that meets the client's deliverable, then test it with the actual booth, lighting, and venue workflow.

## Workflow Steps

1. Install ChackTok and log in.
2. Confirm iPadOS version, camera permission, media permission, storage, and power.
3. Create or clone an event template.
4. Upload branding assets and test the screen layout.
5. Configure capture, processing, sharing, consent, and printing options where used.
6. Run a full test capture.
7. Verify one digital delivery and one print if printing is included.
8. Confirm the fallback plan for interrupted connectivity.

## Processing and Branding

Depending on the selected workflow and app version, ChackTok can support AI background removal and replacement, slow-motion processing, high-frame-rate composition, filters, stickers, frames, overlays, music, and branded graphics.

Client assets and consent wording should be approved before the event.

## Delivery and Reporting

Delivery options can include QR code, email, SMS, gallery access, social sharing, and printing where supported. Eligible upload work can be queued during a network interruption and continue after connectivity returns.

Where enabled and legally appropriate, operators can use guest email capture, social sharing statistics, and engagement analysis in a post-event report. Agree on the reporting scope and guest consent process before the booking.

## FAQ

### Can an iPad booth be used for professional events?

Yes, if the hardware, stand, lighting, event template, branding, and sharing workflow are tested before the event.

### What should operators test first?

Test camera quality, screen orientation, guest flow, gallery access, QR sharing, and battery or charging stability.

### Can an iPad Photo Booth use an external camera?

Yes, where the camera model, connection method, iPad, and app version are compatible. Test the complete capture, transfer, processing, and delivery sequence before a commercial event.

### What happens when venue WiFi fails?

Eligible upload tasks can be queued until connectivity returns. Operators should verify the queue and keep a fallback delivery method rather than relying on immediate online delivery.

### Can the workflow include printing?

Yes, where the iPad, printer, connection method, and app version are compatible. Test paper, consumables, color output, and the reprint process before the booking.
